The case for building ↘
01 / THE IDEA

You can buy
a drone.
You can’t buy
the understanding.

America’s ability to build things starts with people who know how.

Factories matter. Supply chains matter. So do the people who can design, test, repair, and improve the machines. We want to become those people.

A drone is our starting point. Mechanical design, electronics, software, and controls all have to work together. The aircraft is an honest test of whether we understand the connections.

“From scratch” doesn’t mean making every component ourselves. It means taking responsibility for the design, knowing what we’re using, and understanding how to change it.

We’re early. There’s a lot to learn.
That’s a reason to start.
